Planet Earth - Quick guide

Our planet Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life.

Steps to protect our planet

  1. Reduce, Reuse, Recycle: Minimize waste by following the 3 R's.
  2. Conserve Water: Use water wisely and avoid wastage.
  3. Plant Trees: Trees absorb carbon dioxide and provide oxygen.
  4. Use Renewable Energy: Opt for solar, wind, or hydro power.
  5. Support Sustainable Practices: Choose products that are eco-friendly.

incredible Natural Wonders

Key Terms:

Renewable Energy
Energy derived from natural sources that are replenished at a higher rate than they are consumed.
Sustainability
The practice of meeting present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
